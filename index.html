Hello, I'm Sankalp Pandey

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Arkansas, advised by Dr. Khoa Luu. I conduct research in the Quantum AI Lab and the Computer Vision and Image Understanding Lab, where my work focuses on agents and multimodal reasoning for scientific discovery.

I previously received my B.S. in Computer Science and B.S. in Computer Engineering from the University of Arkansas.

Aside from my work, I enjoy tinkering with new ideas, playing tennis, and running trails!
